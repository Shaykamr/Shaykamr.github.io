How We Work

A framework built to move startups from concept to MVP in weeks

Our five-phase co-creation framework runs 12–16 weeks depending on a startup's maturity. Throughout, we function as an extension of the founding team.

01

Weeks 1–3

Discovery & Strategy

Problem validation, market analysis, product requirements documentation, and architecture blueprint.

02

Weeks 3–5

Design & Prototyping

UX research, wireframes, bilingual UI, and usability testing.

03

Weeks 4–10

MVP Development

Full-stack build, API integrations, cloud deployment, and CI/CD.

04

Ongoing

Knowledge Transfer

Technical workshops, mentorship, documentation, and handover preparation, running in parallel throughout.

05

Weeks 11–14

Testing & Handover

QA suite, pilot readiness, sandbox support, and go-live planning.
